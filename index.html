Booking System

How reservations work in StudySpace:

🔍 Find a Spot

Browse available locations near you. Filter by noise, Wi-Fi, outlets, or opening hours. See real-time occupancy.

📅 Select Time

Choose your arrival time and duration (2-4 hours). The system shows available slots in real time.

💳 Confirm & Pay

Free for basic search. €1.50 per reservation at partner locations. No-shows incur a €2 penalty.

€0 - €1.50

📱 QR Check-in

Get a unique QR code. Scan it at the location entrance to confirm your reservation and start your session.
